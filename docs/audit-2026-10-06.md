## Polish list (existing features)

- **Camera:** make mode B the default. Push the speed FOV/shake/wind range to ~65 m/s or put it on a curve. Add a lag spring along the road; today distance is locked to the car's z (`chase_camera.gd:205`). Fix the 120 Hz shake double-scale.
- **Steering:** GEVP's own rate limit (32/v) overrides the player ramp above ~10 m/s, and `steering_exponent` 1.5 halves the speed cap (`gevp_vehicle.gd:670,701`) [code, arithmetic]. Undo the exponent for the player like traffic already does (`traffic_car.gd:159-164`), and assert the real wheel angle in `feel_pass_1`.
- **Handling:** there is no grip fall-off past the tyre peak (`gevp_wheel.gd:263`), so the limit is soft with no snap. There is no understeer test, although build-phases B2 promised one.
- **120 Hz car ≠ 60 Hz car:** 0-100 is 5.18 s vs 5.60 s, braking 43.2 m vs 42.7 m, peak 1.23 g vs 1.30 g [run]. `tick_rate_120` loosened its bounds to fit. Hold the shipped 120 Hz car to the same `chassis_targets`.
- **HUD/palette:** remove the 3 cyan uses (`game.gd:332,368`, `radio_manager.gd:70`, `auto_tune_panel.gd:160`) and the lavender controls line (`game.gd:347`). Add a palette test that scans `scripts/`; today it checks `fleet.json` only.
- **Traffic look:** fade cars in at spawn (about half spawn in plain view between 60 and 150 m). Share meshes and materials per colour. Traffic has no headlights (`traffic_car.gd:297`).
- **Radio (#115):** ducking does nothing on Dale's talk station, but the test asserts it anyway (`tests/audio/radio.gd:158`). Loading a station's 7 tracks hitches the first time you tune it. Dale is captions-only, while the story bible says audio-only.
- **Engine sound (#80):** `cylinders := 4` is fixed and the exhaust preset is hard-coded to `p1_coupe` (`engine_audio.gd:15`), though 12 presets exist. Per-car sound is cheap once more cars exist.
- **Exhaust flames:** the flame events fire (`engine_synth.gd:84-89`), but nothing draws them. A billboard is cheap.

## Broken / risky / contradicts docs

**Broken**
1. In-game Auto-Tune at 120 Hz (#116 fixes it).
2. Traffic + player at speed: the player rear-ends or side-swipes oncoming traffic within seconds [run]. Lanes are **2.3 m** wide (`road_chunk_builder.gd:89`); real highway lanes are 3.5-3.7 m. With ~1.8 m cars that leaves about 0.25 m each side.
3. The controls hint is clipped off-screen [run, screenshot].
4. Typing in the Auto-Tune slot name field triggers T, Y and Esc [code; not run].

**Risky**
- 40-car default against the measured budget (Top 10 #3).
- Every fresh `--editor` run rewrites the tracked `default_bus_layout.tres`: it adds a uid and strips default lines [git diff after this audit's import]. `tests/mute.gd.uid` is untracked; the other 47 test `.uid` files are tracked.
- 74 worktrees, about 640 MB, 57 of them on already-merged branches (not removed).
- GEVP edits have no `.patch` against upstream, and some edited lines carry no `DEVIATION` marker (`brake_mult`, `torque_mult`, `clutch_cap_mult`). The #75 edit is marked "Local change" (`gevp_vehicle.gd:1234`), not DEVIATION as ISSUES.md says.
- A physics-interpolation warning: road MultiMesh transforms are set from `_process` (`road_chunk_builder.gd:757` via `game.gd:384`) [run log]. It may be benign, but it's a jitter suspect.

**Contradictions (flagged, not resolved)**
- **"Full-sim traffic for every car" vs code:** cars beyond the draw distance (150 m default) are frozen kinematic bodies that cruise with no raycast sim (`traffic_car.gd:22-23,184-205`) [code].

- **Default car count budget math:** `traffic_settings.gd:12-15` sizes the 40-car default against the 8.33 ms tick. At 60 fps two 120 Hz ticks run per rendered frame, so the real physics budget is about 4 ms per tick if rendering is to fit. Measured at 40 cars and 150 m: 18-22 cars full-sim, 7-14 ms per tick [run].
- ROADMAP: traffic "NOT started" (stale since #113); "keyboard only", but gamepad bindings exist in the InputMap (digital only); its "Rules carried forward" section is cited by `build-phases.md:51`, but no such section exists.
- ISSUES.md vs GitHub: #18, #55, #73 are listed as closed but are open on GitHub. #62 and #48 are listed as open but are closed. #70 "stop at 200 km/h" conflicts with the 235-250 km/h stock target and Roy's ~300 km/h tuned goal.
