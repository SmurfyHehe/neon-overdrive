## Tone
Dry, tired, working-class. Night shift, sodium light, navy dusk. Humor is deadpan. No cringe old-racer father figure.

## Setting
Harlow Bay, a US port city. Districts: Docks (industrial, dig/roll strips), Downtown (meets, takeovers), Cutter Canyon (touge), Route 9 (freeway runs), the old Airstrip (top-speed pulls).

## The shop

2. Act 1: first wins, first two recruits, Dale starts broadcasting.
3. Act 2: beat each crew's leader and take its district. Mid-act crew drama.
