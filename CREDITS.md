# Credits

Third-party art used in Neon Overdrive. Every vehicle in
`scripts/vehicle_registry.gd` with a `credit` must be listed here;
`tests/vehicle_registry.gd` checks it.

## Vehicles

- **Kenney (www.kenney.nl), Car Kit**: stand-in sedan (stock: `sedan`,
  tuned: `sedan-sports`). Licence: CC0. Source: <https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit>.
  Files: `assets/vehicles/kenney-car-kit/`.

## Code

- **Godot Easy Vehicle Physics** by DAShoe1 (MIT), vendored at
  `scripts/vendor/gevp/`. Source: <https://github.com/DAShoe1/Godot-Easy-Vehicle-Physics>.
