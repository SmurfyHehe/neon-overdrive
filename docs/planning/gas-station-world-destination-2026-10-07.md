# Gas station: world destination and narrative anchor (proposal)

STATUS: proposal only, 2026-10-07. No building until Roy signs off. Names are placeholders (story is Roy's to write).
Roy's steer: the **garage is the primary HQ**. Gas stations are several different locations among the world destinations; they anchor narrative. The environment is "all of the above and more".

Steelman: a destination that is not the HQ can still carry the story, because the player passes through it without choosing to. Premortem: if every station only sells fuel it becomes a menu with a texture; if each one is a bespoke set-piece, cost explodes. So: one reusable kit, three or four dressed variants, one scripted beat each.

## 1. Mechanical purpose
Stations are the fuel stop, but fuel is parked until after Stage E (garage). Design now so it plugs in later with no rework.
- **Refuel**: drive onto the pump apron, stop, hold one key. Cost in currency (Stage C); price varies by district.
- **Quick repairs (later, with damage)**: patch only, never full restore; full restore and mods stay garage-only. This keeps the garage primary.
- **Supplies**: consumables (tyre sealant, coolant, one-run nitrous-free boosts are NOT proposed: gas-only, no gimmicks). Keep to repair kits and tyre swaps.
- **Run-loop role**: a mid-run decision point. Detour costs time and heat; skipping risks a dry tank. Gives Stage C's repeatable loop a risk/reward beat.
- **Not here**: tuning, mod tree, paint. Those are the garage.
Open question for Roy: does a missed stop end the run (empty tank) or only slow the car? Recommend slow-to-limp, not hard fail.

## 2. Narrative anchoring
Stations are where the story talks to the player on the road, using the delivery already in the bible: texts, stills, Dale's broadcasts. No cutscenes.
- **Dunmore Auto link**: the player is a mechanic, so they judge the station's gear and cars. Walt Dunmore's old supplier, now closing. Station owner knows the debt rumour.
- **Vance Ledger**: Ironbridge crew fuels at one specific station (the "Ironbridge pump"); their cars idle there as a standing threat. First pass shows the humiliation setup; later the station is neutral ground or contested.
- **Dale**: the station TV/radio always plays Graveyard TV. Dale's recurring coffee machine bit pays off: the station's coffee machine is also broken. Staff talk about "the voice on the radio".
- **People** (one per station, one line of dialogue per visit, rotating, no menus):
  - Night clerk, behind plexi, deadpan, knows everyone's tab. Tips for favors, like Ferris.
  - Ferris appears at the highway station when his tow truck is parked.
  - Pike Lending collector shows up once, at the wrong time (Act 2 turn).
- Sample lines: "Pump 3 is for decoration." / "Dale says it's the coffee. It's not the coffee."

## 3. World role
| Place | Role | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Garage | Primary HQ: tune, mods, crew, story hub | Safe, personal |
| Gas station | Road-side waypoint: supply, rumours, rival sightings | Transitional, exposed |
Each station is a **story waypoint** (a beat fires on first visit), a **supply point** (fuel/patch), and a light **social spot** (one NPC, ambient crew cars). It is not a hub with a menu.

## 4. Environmental feel (Gritty PS2 night)
- **Light**: sodium canopy pool over the pumps, navy dark beyond it, one flickering tube, buzzing sign. Amber vs. Dusk only; no neon, no magenta/cyan.
- **Weathering**: oil-stained concrete, faded price board with a missing digit, peeling decals, cracked pump glass, taped signs, tyre marks on the apron.
- **Props**: air pump, vending machine, payphone, bin fire barrel, stacked tyres, crooked ice freezer, a dead car on blocks.
- **Audio**: canopy hum, pump clunk, distant traffic, Graveyard TV through the shop window. Vary clips to avoid repetition fatigue.
- **Cars/NPCs**: 1-3 parked NPC cars per variant using the existing NPC set (N1-N3); no pedestrians beyond the clerk silhouette.
- **Cost control**: low-poly kit (canopy, pump, shopfront, sign) reused with palette and prop swaps.

## 5. Design sketch
- **Layout (kit)**: roadside pull-in, 2 pump islands under a canopy, small shop with lit window, side lot with 1-2 props, rear dark service bay. Entry ramps wide enough for the sim cars; exit back to the road.
- **Interaction**: slow into the apron trigger; HUD shows price and a fuel bar (real-car style, no key hints on screen; key is on Controls page). 
- **Story beats**: first visit = clerk line + Dale tie-in; one station per act hosts a scripted beat (see section 6).
- **Mechanics**: refuel, patch, supplies, price by district, heat-aware (cops may camp a station later; police is a separate stage).
- **Style**: PS2-grit, low-res textures, hard shadows off, baked canopy light pool.

## 6. Related destinations (2-3, same kit approach)
1. **Diner / all-night counter** (Downtown): crew meet-up; Juno and Teo scenes; rumours about kings. Warm light, contrast with the gas station.
2. **Tunnel service bay** (Route 9): Okafor, ex-factory tester, offers data-only tips; Dale's signal drops here (radio static beat).

Honourable mention: **Airstrip hangar** for top-speed pulls and the legends reunion.
