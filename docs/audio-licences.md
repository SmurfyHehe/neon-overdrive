# Audio licence log

Every music or sound file that ships in the game gets one row here **before** it
is added to the repo. Keep proof (a screenshot of the licence page and the
download receipt) in `docs/audio-licences-proof/`, named after the `id`.

Order of preference: CC0 (OpenGameArt, itch.io CC0 packs, our own tracks), then
the Pixabay Content License (keep receipts), then Creative Commons BY
(Incompetech and similar: needs a credits entry, and say so in the row if we
trimmed, looped or filtered the track).

**Reject:** non-commercial (NC), no-derivatives (ND), share-alike (SA), GPL,
tracks with no stated licence, and the YouTube Audio Library (only guaranteed
safe for videos on YouTube).

**Not yet read, do not use:** Bensound, Purple Planet, Free Music Archive
(per-track terms), itch.io packs (per-pack terms). Read the licence first and
log it.

Content ID: "royalty-free" tracks can still be claimed by third parties if
gameplay is streamed or uploaded. Keep the proof, and prefer our own generated
tracks for anything in trailers.
